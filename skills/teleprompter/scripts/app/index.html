Pace

Put the words wherever your camera is. You can also drag the lens marker anywhere on the stage for a position in between.

Hold on silence pauses the scroll while you are not speaking, so a pause for breath does not leave you chasing the words.

Background

Gaze

Look straight into the lens and press Calibrate. The meter then scores every frame against that baseline and nudges you when you drift down into the words.

The meter runs live. Correct gaze on a take runs afterwards and writes a new file beside it, so you can play both and keep the one you prefer. It warps the iris toward your lens baseline. It does not repaint the eye, so small corrections hold up and large ones show.
